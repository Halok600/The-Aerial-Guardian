Pipeline

Four stages, one forward pass

No SAHI slicing, no multi-pass tiling — a single high-resolution forward pass through a 4-scale detection head handles targets as small as 8px.

01

Detect — YOLO26-P2

A stride-4 detection level on top of the standard P3/P4/P5 heads — a 240×240 grid at the browser's 960px input — for 8–16px targets that a stride-8 grid barely resolves. Ten VisDrone classes, exported NMS-free for the browser.

10 classes
02

Stabilise — camera motion

Offline, OpenCV's ECC estimates the drone's motion between frames (rotation + translation, coarse-to-fine). In the browser, a translation-only shift is estimated on a 160px thumbnail. Either way, every Kalman prediction is moved with the camera before matching.

Translation-only in-browser
03

Track — ByteTrack

A 3-stage Hungarian association (high-conf → low-conf → tentative tracks) over IoU cost, gated by class group, backed by an 8D constant-velocity Kalman filter per track. A track only gets an ID once it is matched on two frames.

Runs in-browser
04

Render — Labels, trails & counts

Class-group colours, car ID:12-style labels from a majority vote over recent frames, fading trajectory tails, and per-group unique counters.

⚠ Model quality — honest limits

Aerial targets of 8–16px are one of the hardest regimes in object detection, and both models are under-trained: a 9.5-hour Kaggle T4 budget ended the browser model at 50 epochs and the offline model at 24. Expect missed detections, especially of small people.

  • Browser model (YOLO26n-P2 @960) on VisDrone DET-val: person recall 48.8%, precision 73.5%, mAP50 0.429. The offline renders above use the larger s model at 1280px (62.5% / 79.0% / 0.536).
  • Camera-motion compensation in the browser is translation-only (no rotation or zoom); the offline renders and the reported tracking numbers use OpenCV ECC.
  • The browser model returns at most 300 boxes per frame, so very dense scenes lose some.
  • Speed depends on your device and backend (WebGPU or WASM); the FPS meter shows the live rate.
  • Lower the confidence slider if objects never get a track; raise it if you see false positives. It sets the score needed to start a new track.
  • Trained and evaluated on VisDrone only; other altitudes, sensors and scenes were not measured.
